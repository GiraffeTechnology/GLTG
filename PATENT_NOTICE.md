# Patent Notice - GLTG

GLTG is the Giraffe Technology lead-time intelligence engine for apparel and textile execution.

Certain workflows, business methods, system designs, data structures, lead-time simulation mechanisms, path-enumeration mechanisms, reforecasting mechanisms, behavioral adjustment mechanisms, statistical baseline mechanisms, fallback supplier mechanisms, execution-risk guardrails, source-evidence trace mechanisms, Industrial Execution Graph mechanisms, and multi-party C2M / B2M / order-execution workflows implemented or referenced by this project may be covered by patents owned by **Giraffe Technology Holding Limited**.
